## What a moon phase is

The Fondation de la Haute Horlogerie (FHH) defines the moon phase as a **mechanism and display representing the different phases of the moon**. It is a **complication** in FHH's sense — a function beyond the hours, minutes and seconds (see [perpetual calendar](/en/mechanisms/perpetual-calendar/) for another complication in this family).

## How long a lunation takes

FHH's entry states:

- A complete **lunation** takes **29 days, 12 hours, 44 minutes and 2.8 seconds** (per FHH);
- It is divided into **four phases**: **new moon**, **first quarter**, **full moon** and **last quarter** (per FHH).

These are the source's own facts within its entry — this article does not extend them into counting mechanisms, accuracy claims, or comparisons between implementations.
