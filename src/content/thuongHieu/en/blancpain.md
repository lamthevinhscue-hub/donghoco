## Overview

Blancpain calls itself **the world's oldest watchmaking brand**, founded in **1735** in Villeret (Switzerland) by Jehan-Jacques Blancpain. The brand belongs to the Swatch Group and is positioned as the group's **"crown jewel"** — at the top, above both Breguet and Omega.

Its slogan: *"Since 1735, there has never been a quartz Blancpain watch. And there never will be."* — a statement of purely mechanical commitment, made even as the Quartz crisis raged (1983), when Blancpain was among the first brands to return to mechanics.

## Watchmaking philosophy

- **Mechanical only** — no quartz production, rare in the industry.
- **The Villeret tradition** — top-tier hand finishing to the highest standards.
- **Six traditional crafts** (Métiers d'Art) — engraving, enamel, filigree, sculpture and more.
- The brand makes **all of its complicated movements** (calendars, tourbillons, minute repeaters) at its Le Brassus workshop, expanded in 2010 as the R&D and assembly centre for every complicated calibre — a commitment to 100% in-house production.

## Key lines

- **Fifty Fathoms** (1953) — by Blancpain's account, the **first modern dive watch**, developed with French combat swimmer Bob Maloubier and predating the Rolex Submariner by a few months. See the [Fifty Fathoms article](/en/iconic-watches/fifty-fathoms/).
- **Villeret** — the brand's traditional elegant dress-watch line, with white enamel dials and blued steel hands.
- **Classic & Ultra-slim** — pared-down dress watches, a benchmark of restraint.
- **Air Command** — an aviation chronograph (relaunched 2019).
- **Women & Métiers d'Art** — a ladies' and craft-artistry line (Shakudō engraving, enamel, filigree).

## The movements

Notable in-house calibres include the **1315** (automatic, three barrels, 120-hour reserve), the **13R0/13R5** (hand-wound, 8-day reserve) and the **235** (minute repeater/carrousel). Blancpain is also known for its **carrousel** — a tourbillon variant with a double rotation path. In 1991 the brand introduced the "1735" — six grand complications in a single calibre, a statement of peak hand craftsmanship.
