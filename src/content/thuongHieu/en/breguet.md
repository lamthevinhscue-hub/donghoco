## Overview

Breguet is not simply a brand — it is **the name that wrote much of watchmaking's vocabulary**. Breguet hands, Breguet numerals, Breguet overcoil, the tourbillon: four things named after one man that remain industry standards to this day. Abraham-Louis Breguet made watches for the figures who shaped Europe in his lifetime: Empress Marie Antoinette, Napoleon Bonaparte, Tsar Alexander I, the Duke of Wellington. He opened his workshop in 1775 at 51 Quai de l'Horloge on Paris's Île de la Cité; since 1999 the brand has belonged to the Swatch Group.

## Watchmaking philosophy

- **Invention first, decoration second.** Every signature Breguet aesthetic detail was born from a technical need: hollowed tips for fast reading, guilloché dials against glare, pared-down numerals readable by candlelight.
- **Absolute visual identity.** A fluted caseband, a hand-engraved guilloché dial, blued steel apple-tipped hands — three seconds is enough to recognise a Breguet, without seeing the logo.
- **The secret signature.** Every Breguet dial carries a barely visible engraved signature that shows only in raking light — an anti-counterfeiting measure devised by Abraham-Louis Breguet himself and maintained to this day.

## Industry-shaping inventions

Beyond the tourbillon (patented in 1801), Breguet contributed many other important inventions — the striking-work spring for minute repeaters, the pare-chute shock protection, and the overcoil hairspring. The first wristwatch recorded in history — made for the Queen of Naples, ordered in 1810 — also carries his name.

## Key milestones

- **1795** — The tourbillon idea takes shape, six years ahead of the patent.
- **1801** — The tourbillon patent is granted (26 June 1801).
- **1810** — Order no. 2639, for Empress of Naples Caroline Murat — the first recorded wristwatch.
- **1823** — Abraham-Louis Breguet dies; the workshop continues through generations of his family.
- **1999** — Swatch Group acquires Breguet from Investcorp.
- **2023–2024** — The Type XX line returns, followed by gold, blue-dial and ceramic-bezel versions.
