## Overview

Founded in 1839 in Geneva, Patek Philippe is regarded as one of the most prestigious watch brands in the world — a member of the "Holy Trinity" alongside Audemars Piguet and Vacheron Constantin. It has been owned by the Stern family since 1932, now in its fourth generation. Its legendary slogan: *"You never actually own a Patek Philippe. You merely look after it for the next generation."*

## Key milestones

- **1839** — Antoni Patek and François Czapek found the company in Geneva, making fine pocket watches for European aristocracy.
- **1845** — Adrien Philippe joins, bringing his crown-winding invention (winding via the crown instead of a key); the firm becomes Patek Philippe in 1851.
- **1851** — At the Great Exhibition in London, Queen Victoria and Prince Albert buy Patek Philippe pieces — the start of a royal reputation.
- **1925** — The world's first wristwatch perpetual calendar, made for Thomas Packard.
- **1932** — The Stern family acquires the company.
- **1976** — The Nautilus launches (ref. 3700) — the brand's first steel sport-luxury watch, designed by Gérald Genta.
- **1989** — For its 150th anniversary, the Calibre 89 with 33 complications becomes the most complicated watch in the world (1,728 parts) — a record held for 25 years.
- **1996** — The Aquanaut (ref. 5060), a younger take on the Nautilus with a composite strap.
- **2014** — For its 175th anniversary, the Grandmaster Chime — the most complicated Patek ever made (20 complications, two faces); only seven pieces.
- **2022** — Production of the steel Nautilus 5711 ends, turning the reference into a collecting phenomenon.

## Watchmaking philosophy

Patek Philippe pursues **absolute finishing**. Since 2009, the brand has applied its own **Patek Philippe Seal** — a stricter internal standard than the Geneva Seal, covering the finished watch (not just the movement). Every watch must meet demanding technical and aesthetic criteria, and the company makes all of its own movements, from simple calibres to some of the most complicated in the world.

## Key lines

- **Nautilus** — steel sport-luxury designed by Gérald Genta; the most sought-after Patek line.
- **Aquanaut** — a more modern, younger take on the Nautilus.
- **Calatrava** — the classic elegant dress watch, a benchmark of restraint.
- **Grand Complications** — perpetual calendars, minute repeaters, tourbillons.
