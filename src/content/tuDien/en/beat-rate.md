**Beat rate** — usually written as **vph** (vibrations per hour) — describes the **frequency of the oscillator** in a mechanical watch.

## Units describing the same beat

Per the Fondation de la Haute Horlogerie (FHH):

- **Frequency** is **the number of oscillations per second, measured in Hertz (Hz)**.
- **Each oscillation** — one to-and-fro movement of the balance — **comprises two vibrations**.

So `Hz` counts per second and `vph` counts vibrations per hour: two ways of naming the same beat of the regulating organ.

## Frequency and accuracy — per FHH

FHH's *Frequency* entry states verbatim: **"The higher the frequency, the more accurate the watch: 21,600 vibrations/hour (3 Hz), 28,800 vibrations/hour (4 Hz) and 36,000 vibrations/hour (5 Hz)."** — the higher the frequency, the more accurate the watch, with the three levels named in the entry: 21,600 vph (3 Hz), 28,800 vph (4 Hz) and 36,000 vph (5 Hz).

This is the source's own statement within its entry — this article does not extend it into comparisons between levels, and attaches no level to any brand or product line.
