The **escape wheel** is **one of the three parts of the escapement** — alongside the lever and the roller, per the *Assortiment* entry of the Fondation de la Haute Horlogerie (FHH).

## Where it sits

FHH defines the escapement as the mechanism **fitted between the gears and the regulating organ**, whose function is to **suspend the gears' motion at regular intervals** and to **supply energy to the balance**. Within that mechanism, the escape wheel is the part interfacing with the gear train: instead of spinning freely, its motion is **locked and released in even steps**, following the beat of the oscillator.

## How the terms connect

- The whole mechanism is the **[escapement](/en/mechanisms/escapement/)**.
- The energy the escapement supplies reaches the **[balance & hairspring](/en/mechanisms/balance-and-hairspring/)** — the watch's regulating organ.
- The energy flows from the mainspring through the gear train to this point — see [how a mechanical watch works](/en/mechanisms/how-a-mechanical-watch-works/).

The familiar "tick-tock" belongs to the oscillator's beat: per FHH, each to-and-fro movement of the balance is one oscillation, comprising two vibrations.
