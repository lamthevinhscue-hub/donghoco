**GMT** (short for *Greenwich Mean Time*) is the complication that **displays a second time zone** — letting you read the hour in another zone (usually home time while you travel).

## How to recognize it

- **A GMT hand** distinguished by its own design or colour, completing one turn per 24 hours.
- **A 24-hour scale**, usually on the bezel; on some models the bezel is rotatable, often split into two colours to distinguish the day half from the night half.

## Why 24 hours?

The GMT hand completes one turn per 24 hours (not 12) so that you can **tell day from night**. The hand on "8" means 8 in the morning; "20" means 8 in the evening.

## A short history

GMT became popular through the **Rolex GMT-Master**, launched in **1955** according to Rolex — it later became the official watch of the airline Pan American World Airways (Pan Am), serving travellers who needed both local time and home time.
