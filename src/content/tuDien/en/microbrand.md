**A microbrand** is a small, independently owned watch company that assembles its watches from **bought-in components** — the movement, case, dial, hands, crystal and strap — and **sells them directly to the end customer**, mainly through the brand's own website and very rarely through the traditional retail network.

The point to keep in mind: a microbrand **designs watches but does not manufacture the components inside them**. The value of the model sits in the design work, in the careful selection of bought-in parts, and in the direct relationship with the customer — not in in-house production.

## What is bought in, what is the brand's own

- **Bought in**: the movement, case, dial, hands, crystal and strap — the physical components.
- **The brand's own**: the design of the watch, the choice among suppliers' parts, and the direct line to the customer.

A full analysis of this model — what microbrands actually do, what they deliberately leave to suppliers, what the buyer gains, and what the risks are — is covered in a separate guide on this site, currently available in Vietnamese only ("Microbrand là gì — mô hình đang thay đổi thị trường đồng hồ cơ giá vừa").
