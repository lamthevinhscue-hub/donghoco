The **minute repeater** is a **complication** that strikes the time. Per the Fondation de la Haute Horlogerie (FHH), a repeater is a watch that **strikes the time on demand** by activating a **pushpiece or a slide**; the minute repeater precisely indicates the **number of minutes elapsed**, and the first such mechanisms appeared in the **early years of the 18th century (1700–1710)**, for the most part in southern Germany.

How FHH distinguishes repeaters from the sonnerie family → [minute repeater](/en/mechanisms/minute-repeater/), and another complication → [tourbillon](/en/mechanisms/tourbillon/).
