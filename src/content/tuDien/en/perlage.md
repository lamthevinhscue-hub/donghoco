**Perlage** is a surface finish consisting of **overlapping concentric circles**. In FHH's list of movement surface treatments, this technique is described as **stippling (spotting)**, and the source records parts as "circular-grained" (per FHH).

## The term's scope

In the Poinçon de Genève's published requirements for baseplates, plates for additional mechanisms and bridges, the **recesses (creusures)** must receive a "perlées" (circular-graining) finish — **or another finish** that likewise eliminates all machining marks (per the Poinçon de Genève). The standard thus ties this finish to **specific component locations** and to the stated aim of eliminating machining marks, not to an overall quality level.

Perlage names a surface treatment — it is not a certification, and not an overall quality assessment of a watch.
