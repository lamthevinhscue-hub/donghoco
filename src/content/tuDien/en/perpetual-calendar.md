The **perpetual calendar** (French: *quantième perpétuel*) is a **calendar complication**. Per the Fondation de la Haute Horlogerie (FHH), it is devised to incorporate the **specificities of the Gregorian calendar**: it **automatically adjusts to months with 30 days and to the 28 or 29 days in February**, thanks to a **mechanical memory whose sequences are repeated every 48 months** to correspond to the cycle of leap years.

## How it differs from an annual calendar

FHH defines the **annual calendar** as a simple calendar that accounts for **months with less than 31 days but not leap years**, and that **must be adjusted once a year**. The two are **different mechanisms** — this article does not rank them; each model's documentation says which one it uses.

## The exceptional adjustments — per FHH

A perpetual calendar must only be adjusted for the **exceptionally non-leap years — the next being 2100 and 2400** (per FHH).
