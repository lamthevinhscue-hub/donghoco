The **tourbillon** is a **complication**: per the Fondation de la Haute Horlogerie (FHH), a system **devised and patented by Abraham-Louis Breguet in 1801** to **compensate for errors of rate caused by the Earth's gravitational force in upright positions** — the escapement mounted in a **revolving cage** with the balance at the centre. The cage **generally revolves once a minute**.
